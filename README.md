# power-BI-E-commerce-analysis
The 2025 performance data reveal an e-commerce business powered by exceptional core margins, a resilient revenue distribution, and a massive Q4 holiday surge driven by transaction frequency.
1. The Profit Engine: High Margins Meet Mass Volume
While Sports & Outdoors claims the profitability crown with a 42.53% margin, Electronics serves as the primary financial workhorse of the business. Moving over 15,000 units, Electronics avoids the common trap of sacrificing margin for scale, it maintains an impressive 41.53% margin, proving that high-volume catalog items are actively building bottom-line profit rather than acting as low-margin loss leaders.

2. Customer Health: A Well-Balanced Revenue Base
Rather than relying on a fragile, top-heavy buyer base, the storefront exhibits a healthy spread of purchasing power. The top 10% of customer’s accounts for 32.98% of total revenue ($1.07M out of $3.24M). This baseline confirms solid VIP customer retention without leaving the brand dangerously over-indexed on a tiny handful of accounts.

3. The Return Myth: Debunking Quality Concerns
Scatter plot analysis disproved the conventional wisdom that low product ratings drive merchandise returns. Return rates hold steady at a 4.67% baseline across all rating tiers, with 5-star products experiencing high-return spikes just as frequently as 3-star items. This establishes that product quality isn't the root cause of returns; instead, operational post-purchase friction such as sizing mismatches, shipping delays, or impulse buyer’s remorse, is the real culprit.

4. The Q4 Surge: Channel Dynamics During Peak Season
Q4 delivers a massive 79.9% revenue leap (jumping from $684.8K in Q3 to $1.23M in Q4), propelled by sheer order volume. While Web ($548.7K) and Mobile App ($521.6K) process the bulk of holiday dollars, Marketplace emerges as the growth catalyst with a +90.3% surge. Capturing third-party platform traffic allows the brand to intercept holiday shoppers who bypass owned channels during peak buying periods.

Strategic Roadmap
To maximize next year's playbook: scale Marketplace ad spends ahead of Q4, refine post-purchase fulfillment and sizing guides rather than altering product cataloging to curb returns, and cross-sell high-margin Sports & Outdoors products directly to the massive Electronics buyer audience.
